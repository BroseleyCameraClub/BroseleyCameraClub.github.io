#### Presentation

<br>

The presentation on *'Textures'* was held.

This month we had a quick workshop about textures and how the light can adjust the look and feel of an object. Members had the opportunity to take control of a light and move this around to see how this effects the contrast and detail of different objects.

It was encouraged that members try this at home with different light sources.


<p class="prog-SubDesc">Members receive a copy of the presentation in the club newsletter.</p>
