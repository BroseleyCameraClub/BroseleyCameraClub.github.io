Welcome note back from the Xmas period and looking forward to the next season's programme. We discussed a few images that were not entered by members for other competitions, and trips. The competitions were run through and then a selection of images that members were 'going' to enter were critiqued - This gave some insight to what our peers would look for - some, unfortunately, would of received more votes than they thought. Great feedback, and a good learning experience.
